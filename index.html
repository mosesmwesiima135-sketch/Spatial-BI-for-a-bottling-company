About this demo and how the real platform would work

Every number on this page is synthetic, generated to show how a bottler's sales, distribution, cooler, competitor and weather data could sit on one map. Towns, plants and depots are placed at approximate real locations; lake outlines are simplified; regions are approximate.

Analysis unit: ~20 km hexagons (in production, H3 hexagons or parishes). Production stack: outlet geo-census (KoboToolbox/QField) and ERP/SFA sales feeds → Python/R pipelines → PostgreSQL/PostGIS warehouse → Bayesian small-area models (R-INLA) for demand and stock-out risk → vector-tile and API layer → this kind of web dashboard, with role-based access per region.

Open data that would feed it: UBOS census 2024 and population grids (WorldPop), building footprints, VIIRS night-time lights (spending power), CHIRPS rainfall and temperature (weather demand), OpenStreetMap roads (routing and travel time).

Boundaries: Natural Earth (via world-atlas). Map tiles not used; all geometry is embedded. Synthetic data, for demonstration only.
